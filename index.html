ורמינהו: מצותה עם הנץ החמה, כדי שיסמוך גאולה לתפלה, ונמצא מתפלל ביום!

A little context before you start

The Mishnah allows the morning Amidah until midday; Rabbi Yehudah says until four hours into the day. Here the Gemara brings a teaching about sunrise. How do the teachings fit together?

The teaching discusses finishing Shema and its blessings just before the Amidah. מצותה refers to Shema.
