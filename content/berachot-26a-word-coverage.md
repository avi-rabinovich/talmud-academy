# Word quizzes for the rest of Berachot 26a, chapter 4

Status: editorial draft for educator review. Source: [Bavli Berachot 26a](https://www.sefaria.org/Berakhot.26a). The English teaching material is original; spellings and abbreviations in the passages follow the displayed daf.

The existing opening vocabulary quiz covers ורמינהו through the statement about ותיקין. These three quizzes continue immediately afterward, ending at שמע מינה. This completes vocabulary coverage of the chapter-4 discussion on this amud; it does not include the preceding chapter's material at the top of 26a or the next-page catchword תנו.

## 4. Mistake, answer, and proof — 19 questions

Passage: מיתיבי through ש״מ at the end of the amud.

Terms: מיתיבי, מעות, לא יוכל לתקון, וחסרון, להמנות, זה שבטל, ק״ש, או, שנמנו חביריו, לדבר מצוה, ולא נמנה עמהם, הכא במאי עסקינן, שבטל במזיד, דיקא נמי, דקתני, ולא קתני, טעה, ש״מ. One additional question tests the function of הכא במאי עסקינן as a clarification answering the objection.

Each quiz has a study list, the complete corresponding passage, four-choice questions, explanations, 10 points per correct question, and practice for missed questions. Correct answer positions vary. Signal meanings and discussion functions are distinguished. Proper names are not tested as vocabulary; recurring connectors are taught within phrases.

`vocabulary-rest.js` is the source question bank, including distractors and feedback. `word-quizzes.js` delivers it. Completion means finishing a round, while mastery and best score reflect correct answers, including subsequent practice. Existing browser progress keeps its original indices; new vocabulary activities are inserted before the relevant later parsing activities without erasing prior scores.
