# Parsing quiz 3: a mistake or a deliberate omission?

Status: editorial draft for educator review. Source: [Bavli Berachot 26a](https://www.sefaria.org/Berakhot.26a), מיתיבי through שמע מינה at the end of the amud. Printed abbreviations follow the scan.

This continues quiz 2, without repeating its exchanges. Three sections earn 10 points each:

1. **Objection:** מיתיבי … ולא נמנה עמהם. Include the verse and the entire quoted teaching, including its second part about missing participation in a mitzvah. Although the prayer clause raises the immediate difficulty, this exercise asks for the complete quotation as one unit.
2. **Answer:** א״ר יצחק א״ר יוחנן … שבטל במזיד. Include the attribution and the clarification that the quoted teaching concerns deliberate omission. The earlier discussion concerned a mistaken omission.
3. **Support:** אמר רב אשי … ש״מ. Isolate Rav Ashi’s inference from בטל rather than טעה, including its acceptance. This is support for the answer, not a new objection.

The printed תנו at the foot of 26a is a catchword previewing the next page. It is included as one selectable overshoot word but excluded from the correct final section. The exercise finishes at the natural end of this discussion, before the next page’s teaching.

Key signals: מיתיבי (objection), הכא במאי עסקינן (clarifying the case), דיקא נמי (support from precise wording), דקתני (for it teaches), ש״מ / שמע מינה (accept the inference).
