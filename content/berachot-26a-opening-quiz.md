# Berachot 26a: the opening exchange

Status: first editorial draft; not yet educator-reviewed.

Audience: children around age 11 beginning Gemara, with some Mishnah background.

Source: [Bavli Berakhot 26a](https://www.sefaria.org/Berakhot.26a), chapter 4, opening Gemara, from ורמינהו through the statement about ותיקין finishing at sunrise. Ancient Hebrew/Aramaic text; English questions and explanations below are original teaching material.

## Before the quiz

The Mishnah says that the morning prayer may be said until midday; Rabbi Yehudah says until four hours into the day. The Gemara brings a teaching that appears to require prayer at sunrise. It then explains that the teaching describes the practice of the ותיקין, people especially careful in performing mitzvot.

Here, תפלה refers to the Amidah. The teaching discusses finishing Shema and its blessings so that the blessing of redemption leads directly into the Amidah.

## Passage

> ורמינהו: מצותה עם הנץ החמה, כדי שיסמוך גאולה לתפלה, ונמצא מתפלל ביום!
>
> כי תניא ההיא — לותיקין.
>
> דאמר רבי יוחנן: ותיקין היו גומרים אותה עם הנץ החמה.

Punctuation and line breaks are teaching aids. Display the passage while students answer. The answer key should be hidden in a student-facing version.

## Questions

### 1. What does ורמינהו tell us here?

A. Now we will tell a story.
B. Here is another teaching that seems to disagree.
C. Everyone agrees with this answer.
D. We have finished the discussion.

### 2. What does הנץ החמה mean?

A. Sunset
B. Midday
C. Midnight
D. Sunrise

### 3. What does כדי mean here?

A. In order to
B. Even though
C. Instead of
D. Afterward

### 4. What does שיסמוך mean here?

A. That he should repeat
B. That he should leave out
C. That he should place next to
D. That he should ask about

### 5. What does ביום mean?

A. At night
B. During the day
C. Before sunrise
D. On the next day

### 6. What does כי תניא ההיא mean here?

A. Who disagreed with that teaching?
B. That teaching was never taught.
C. When that teaching was taught…
D. Why was that teaching forgotten?

### 7. What is כי תניא ההיא — לותיקין doing in this discussion?

A. Answering by explaining whom the earlier teaching is about
B. Asking for the meaning of an unfamiliar word
C. Bringing a new objection from the Mishnah
D. Starting a story about a different subject

### 8. Who are the ותיקין in this passage?

A. People who have never learned Mishnah
B. People who missed the morning prayer
C. People who are travelling at night
D. People especially careful in performing mitzvot

### 9. What does דאמר mean here, before רבי יוחנן?

A. But he asked
B. For he said
C. If he forgot
D. Until he returned

### 10. Why does the Gemara bring Rabbi Yochanan's statement here?

A. To introduce an unrelated rule about evening prayer
B. To show that the Mishnah has no time limit
C. To support the explanation about the ותיקין
D. To ask who wrote the Mishnah

### 11. What does היו גומרים mean?

A. They used to finish
B. They were about to ask
C. They refused to begin
D. They forgot to say

## Answer key and feedback

| Question | Answer | Feedback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | B | ורמינהו introduces a clash between teachings. Here, sunrise seems to clash with the Mishnah's later deadline. |
| 2 | D | הנץ החמה means sunrise. |
| 3 | A | כדי introduces a purpose: in order to do something. It does not introduce an objection here. |
| 4 | C | שיסמוך means placing things next to each other. Here, the blessing of redemption comes directly before the Amidah. |
| 5 | B | ביום means during the day. The ב־ here means “in” or “during.” |
| 6 | C | כי תניא ההיא means “When that teaching was taught…” The next word explains whom it concerns. |
| 7 | A | The answer explains that the sunrise teaching concerns the practice of the ותיקין. |
| 8 | D | Here, ותיקין describes people especially careful in performing mitzvot, including the timing of prayer. |
| 9 | B | דאמר means “for he said” here. It introduces Rabbi Yochanan's supporting statement. |
| 10 | C | Rabbi Yochanan describes the ותיקין finishing Shema at sunrise, supporting the answer just given. |
| 11 | A | היו גומרים means “they used to finish.” Here it refers to finishing Shema. |

## Teacher notes

- Priority discussion signals: ורמינהו (objection), כי תניא ההיא (introduces a contextual explanation here), דאמר (introduces support here).
- Questions 1, 6, and 9 check signal meanings; questions 7 and 10 separately check their role in the passage.
- Teach the sequence: apparent contradiction → explanation → supporting statement.
- Do not teach that every question is an objection or that every occurrence of אמר introduces an answer.
- The word מצותה refers to Shema in the cited teaching, not directly to the Amidah. Supply this context before testing the passage.
- This initial quiz selects key vocabulary; it does not yet test every word in the passage.
- Review age suitability, translations, distractor clarity, and source interpretation with a knowledgeable educator before publication.
